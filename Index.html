Privacy Policy for Briknova

This Privacy Policy explains how Briknova ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you install and play the Briknova Android mobile game. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the minimum information required to deliver gameplay and support advertising through Google AdMob.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Briknova is an offline-capable single-player arcade game and does not require account registration, login credentials, or personal profiles on our servers. We do not operate remote developer servers to collect or store your personal data.

The following game data is stored strictly locally on your device:

  • Game progress, unlocked levels, stars earned, and achievements.
  • Local Top 10 leaderboard scores and the player display nickname you choose in the app.
  • Virtual in-game currency (coins), unlocked paddle/ball/trail skins, and daily reward streak state.
  • Audio, haptics, and gameplay difficulty preferences.

You can delete all locally stored game data at any time from within the app (Settings > Reset Local Game Progress or Leaderboard > Clear Scores) or by uninstalling the App.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Consent Services (Google AdMob & UMP)

Briknova displays advertisements (banner ads on the main menu, interstitial ads between game sessions, and optional rewarded ads) provided by Google AdMob and uses the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage regional privacy consent requirements (such as GDPR in the EEA/UK and state privacy laws in the US).

When your device is connected to the internet, Google Mobile Ads and UMP may automatically collect and process certain data to deliver, personalize (where permitted), cap frequency, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, including:

  • Advertising Identifier: Your device's Google Advertising ID (AD_ID), subject to your device settings and consent choices.
  • Approximate Location / Network Data: IP address used to determine general region for regional consent compliance and ad delivery.
  • Device & App Diagnostics: Device model, OS version, app version, crash/performance logs, and ad interaction events (such as ad impressions and clicks).

For details on how Google uses information from apps that use its advertising services, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Data Protection

3. App Permissions

Briknova requests only essential permissions needed for core operation and ad delivery:

  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to check connectivity and load ads from Google AdMob.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising and fraud prevention in accordance with Google Play policies.

Briknova does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise GPS location, SMS, call logs, or external file storage.

4. Your Privacy Choices & Opt-Out Controls

  • Manage Ad Consent (UMP): Where applicable (such as in the EEA, UK, or regulated regions), you can review or update your ad consent preferences at any time inside Briknova via Settings > Manage Ad Consent (UMP).
  • Reset or Opt Out of Advertising ID: You can limit ad personalization or delete/reset your Advertising ID in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads depending on your Android version).
  • Offline Play: Core gameplay works offline without loading network ads.

5. Children's Privacy

Briknova is a general-audience arcade game and is not specifically directed to children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services in connection with the App, please contact us so we can assist promptly.

6. Data Security

Local game records remain in your device's private app storage sandbox. Any network requests made by Google Mobile Ads and UMP are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates, SDK changes, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
